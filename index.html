🎯
Building the Kole404 frame
▾

That look is lean + functional — not bulky. At your height and current weight you are close to the frame already. The gap is muscle density, especially shoulders and upper back. Don't bulk aggressively.

1
Barbell overhead press — your single most important exercise
Heavy, every week, progressive. This builds shoulder width and the entire upper frame look.
2
Dumbbell lateral raise — done slowly
3–5s eccentrics only. This is what builds the shoulder cap that makes you look wide from the front.
3
Barbell bent-over row
Back thickness + posture. Good posture alone changes how your frame looks dramatically.
4
Barbell squat
Prevents the top-heavy only look. Also the biggest testosterone-releasing exercise in your arsenal.
🤸
Handstand push-up — full progression
▾

The strength is easier than the skill. Do 5–12 min of handstand practice every single day — even rest days. Skill needs daily repetition, not occasional heavy sessions.

Mo 1–2
Pike push-up + wall kick-up hold
Learn the pattern. Wall hold: even 2–3 seconds counts.
Mo 3–4
Elevated pike push-up + controlled negatives (5s)
Feet on chair for more shoulder load. Negatives build the push-up strength.
Mo 5–6
Partial HSPU (1/3 to 1/2 range)
Deepen the range each week. Wall hold at 45s+.
Mo 7–8
Full wall HSPU — first rep achieved
Nose to floor, push back up. Build from 1 rep to 8+ reps.
Mo 10–12
Freestanding handstand hold + HSPU attempts
Away from wall. 3–5 second holds → full freestanding HSPU is the ultimate goal beyond 12 months.

Hair
Work with the waves
Wavy hair is dry by nature — oils can't travel down the wave pattern easily. Everything that causes frizz comes from dryness. Fix the moisture and the waves define themselves.
Wash days (3×/week)
Never shampoo daily — strips natural oils
Step 1
Sulfate-free shampoo — scalp only, not the lengths
Step 2
Conditioner — mid-lengths to ends · 2–3 min · Rinse with cool water only
Step 3
Scrunch upward with old T-shirt or microfiber towel · Never regular towel
Step 4
Leave-in conditioner while still wet · Scrunch up · Air dry · Don't touch while drying
Non-wash days
Co-wash or water refresh
Option 1
Co-wash — conditioner only, no shampoo · Cleanses without stripping
Option 2
Water refresh — wet hands, scrunch into hair · Apply small leave-in · Air dry
Comb
Wide-tooth only · Wet hair with conditioner in only · Never brush dry
Oil
1–2 drops argan or coconut on ends only · Not scalp · Seals moisture

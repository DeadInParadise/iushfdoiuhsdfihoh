Every goal addressed
Each problem broken down with its cause, solution, and exactly what to do about it.
💪
Thin wrists — no forearm definition
▾

Wrists are mostly bone and tendon — they don't bulk up. But the forearm muscles that wrap around them grow visibly, and lower body fat makes veins surface. At 72kg / 5'11" you're lean enough — it's a muscle-fill problem.

The 7 exercises that fix this
1
Gripper (150lbs) — daily habit
3 sets × max reps each hand · Every single day, even rest days · Squeeze fully, 3-count release
2
Dumbbell hammer curl
3×12 · Neutral grip (thumb up) · Hits brachialis + brachioradialis — the muscles that make forearms thick and vascular
3
Wrist curl — palm up
3×15 · Forearm on thigh, curl wrist up · Inner forearm development
4
Reverse wrist curl — palm down
3×15 · Outer forearm — makes arm look wider from front
5
DIY wrist roller
Tie 2kg weight to barbell with 1m rope · Hold barbell at shoulder height · Roll weight up and down × 3 rounds · Brutal and highly effective
6
Plate pinch hold
Pinch 2kg plate with fingers × 30–45s holds · 3 sets · Builds finger and wrist tendon thickness directly
7
Barbell deadlift
3×8 every pull day · The single best compound exercise for forearm and grip development — nothing else comes close
🎯
No strong frame — the Kole404 physique
▾

That look is lean + functional — not bulky. At your height and weight you're already close to the frame. The gap is muscle density — especially shoulders, upper back, and forearms. You don't need to bulk aggressively.

1
Barbell overhead press — your #1 exercise
4×6–8 heavy · Most important for shoulder width and the overall "frame" look · Progress this every week
2
Dumbbell lateral raise — done correctly
4×15 · Slow and controlled only. Swinging is wasted time. This shapes the shoulder cap that makes you look wide from the front.
3
Barbell bent-over row — back thickness
4×8 · Good posture from a strong back alone changes how your frame looks dramatically
4
Floor press — chest development
3×8 safely without a rack · Chest + triceps
5
Barbell squat — don't skip legs
3×10 minimum every week · Prevents the "top-heavy only" look · Also releases testosterone
🤸
Handstand push-up — full progression
▾

The strength is the easier part. The harder part is balance and body awareness inverted. Do 5 minutes every single day regardless of whether you're training.

Wk 1–4
Pike push-up (feet on floor) + wall kick-up hold
3×8 pike · Wall hold: 3× max (even 2s is fine) · Teaches movement pattern and wrist stability
Wk 5–8
Elevated pike push-up (feet on chair) + wall hold 15s+
More vertical angle = more shoulder load · Start first negatives: kick up, lower slowly 3–5s, drop
Wk 8–12
Controlled negatives (5–8s) + partial range HSPU
Kick up, lower head to floor in 5–8s. 5 reps. Then partial: go 1/3 down, push back. Add range weekly.
Wk 10+
Full wall HSPU attempts
Full range — nose to floor and back up. Most people hit this between week 10–16 with consistent daily practice.
✦
Fat cheeks — undefined jaw
▾

You cannot spot-reduce face fat. But as you build muscle your body composition improves and face fat naturally reduces. The biggest offender: high sodium (puffy face). Reduce salt immediately.

1
Mewing — tongue posture (all day, every day)
Tongue flat on roof of mouth at all times. This is a posture habit. Results at 6–12 months.
2
Jaw chewing — hard foods
Sugarfree mastic gum or hard fruits (apple, carrot) daily · Exercises masseter muscle · More defined jaw angle over months
3
Chin tucks — 3×10 daily
Pull chin straight back (not down), hold 5s · Strengthens neck, improves jawline from side and front
4
Cut sodium + drink 3L water
Less salt = less face puffiness — visible difference in 2–3 weeks

Hair
Work with the waves
Wavy hair is dry by nature — the natural oils from your scalp can't travel down the wave pattern easily. Everything that causes frizz comes from dryness. Fix the dryness, the waves define themselves.
Wash days (3×/week max)
Never wash daily — strips natural oils
Step 1
Sulfate-free shampoo — massage scalp only, not lengths
Step 2
Conditioner — mid-lengths to ends only · Leave 2–3 min · Rinse with cool water (seals cuticle = less frizz)
Step 3
Scrunch upward with old T-shirt or microfiber towel · Never rub with regular towel
Step 4
Leave-in conditioner while hair is still wet · Scrunch upward · Air dry only — never blow dry without diffuser
Step 5
Don't touch while drying · Once dry, scrunch to break cast if you used gel
Non-wash days
Co-wash or just water refresh
Option 1
Co-wash — conditioner only wash, no shampoo · Cleanses without stripping
Option 2
Water refresh — wet hands, scrunch water into hair · Apply small amount leave-in · Scrunch and air dry
Comb
Wide-tooth comb only · Only when wet with conditioner · Never brush dry hair
Oil
1–2 drops argan or coconut oil on ends only (not scalp) · Seals moisture in
